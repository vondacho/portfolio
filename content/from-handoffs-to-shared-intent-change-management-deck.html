CHANGE MANAGEMENT

FROM HANDOFFS
TO SHARED INTENT.

Accompanying traditional BA, QA and Development teams into Contract-Driven Development
The transformation is not from people to automation.
It is from sequential interpretation to shared, executable understanding.
Frame this as a people-and-practices transformation. The technology is useful, but the real change is when BA, QA and Development meet around shared intent before ambiguity becomes expensive.
The change is collaboration, not automation.

QUALITY ASSURANCE

QA moves into agreement formation. Emphasize that shift-left does not eliminate exploratory, integrated or production-oriented testing.
QA challenges the agreement early and still tests reality.
DEVELOPMENT

From interpreting requirements to implementing agreements

CDD should reduce ambiguity—not become another approval layer before coding.

BEFORE

Read requirement → Interpret → Implement → Discover misunderstanding

WITH SHARED AGREEMENTS

Explore intent → Agree behavior → Make selected expectations executable → Implement independently → Verify continuously

The developer still designs the solution. The expected outcome becomes clearer.
Protect developer autonomy. Contracts clarify externally important outcomes; they should not prescribe internal implementation unnecessarily.
Clearer outcomes, preserved design autonomy.
THE TEAM MODEL

Roles remain distinct. Boundaries become more permeable.

Each discipline brings a different question into the shared space.

BA

Are we solving the right problem?

DEV

Can we design and build this coherently?

QA

What evidence would convince us—including when things go wrong?

The shared space is where intent becomes agreement.
This is the role model: different questions, shared space. Avoid implying everyone becomes a generic product engineer.
Distinct expertise, permeable boundaries.

GUIDED PRACTICE

Learn through real delivery

Use pairing across disciplines instead of relying on classroom transformation.

BA + QA

Clarify rules, examples and exceptions.

BA + DEV

Explore domain meaning and solution constraints.

DEV + QA

Turn scenarios into executable verification.

BA + QA + DEV

Agree what “done” means.

Build capability through guided practice, not methodology deployment.
Pairing creates capability through work. Coaches or experienced practitioners should accompany early iterations, then step back.
Guided practice beats classroom rollout.

Reward shared outcomes.
